# SC Web Transitions 3.2.5: Organic Frost (private test build)

## Ice OUT
- Removed the straight-edged top and bottom freeze wipes.
- Replaced them with a deterministic multi-source growth field that follows the keyed human silhouette.
- Frost starts at staggered top and bottom contact seeds, propagates through variable-resistance regions, grows faster along meandering crystal fingers and branches, and meets in a non-linear seam.
- Adds subtle bright crystalline edges while the frost grows.
- Freeze holds before slow cracks, then the existing silhouette shards explode outward.

## Ice IN
- Preserves the approved 3.2.4 rewind: broken fragments fly back, form an ice silhouette, crack, and shatter to uncover the live camera.
- ICE IN has no rectangular freeze-growth wipe; only Ice OUT's formation was changed.

## Unchanged
Money IN, Money OUT, Black Smoke, Toxic Cloud, OBS transition controller, hotkeys, and existing installer behavior.

## Notes
This uses a precomputed 2D crystal propagation and a keyed still frame. It is not a physically simulated volumetric ice solver, continuous live-alpha tracker, or validated native OBS plugin.
