# SC Web Transitions v3.12.1 — Ice Sparkle Crack Flash RC1

Base: v3.12.0 Ice Shatter Luxury Spin.

## Focus
Further polish **Ice Shatter** with brighter crystal sparkle and a more suspenseful pre-explosion crack sequence.

## What changed
- Added stronger **sparkling crystal highlights** in both the 2D fallback and WebGL2 3D material path.
- Added a slower **pre-explosion crack buildup** so the frozen figure builds tension before shattering.
- Added a stronger **frost crack flash** right before the blast, plus a short residual flash at the start of the breakup.
- Slightly extended the **Ice OUT break timing** to allow more anticipation.
- Preserved the upgraded airborne shard rotation, lift and wobble from v3.12.0.
- Preserved Money CGI Ultra and Money IN luxury improvements.

## Intended result
The ice should now feel more premium and theatrical: viewers see the cracks slowly spreading, then a bright crack flash, then the frozen body explodes into rotating shards with more sparkle.
